Frequently Asked Questions

GreenGrocer is an online marketplace that offers a wide selection of fresh produce, pantry staples, and other grocery items delivered right to your doorstep.

GreenGrocer is committed to sustainability by partnering with local farmers, using eco-friendly packaging, and ensuring the highest quality products.

GreenGrocer currently operates in select locations. You can check our service availability by entering your zip code on our website.

GreenGrocer was established in 2024, with the mission to make fresh and healthy food accessible to everyone. The web-based application was created as part of a project at Hofstra University in the grueling Software Engineering class of an infamous professor Who-Shall-Not-Be-Named.

We source our products from kroger's stores in partnership with local farms, trusted suppliers, and certified organic producers to ensure freshness and quality.
